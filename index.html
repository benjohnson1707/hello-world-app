Overview

The framework organises the capabilities a Technical Program Manager needs across 5 domains and 4 lifecycle phases, reviewed through 6 review questions. Records connect in a chain:

Capability→Skill ID→Application ID→Deliverable & acceptance evidence

Each application links to one Coverage ID and one proposed Workflow ID.
